I’ve always had a strong interest in mugs - they’ve been a small but constant source of inspiration in my everyday life. I’m especially drawn to the unique, slightly imperfect ones you find in second-hand shops, the kinds with unexpected shapes, hand-painted details, and those soft, floral patterns that feel both nostalgic and personal. There’s something about them that feels more meaningful than mass-produced pieces, like each one carries its own story.

At home, mugs are kind of a big deal. We have so many of them, mostly because my mum loves them too, and over time they’ve just collected into this mix of colours, textures, and styles. Everyone in the house has their own favourites that we naturally “claim,” and it’s funny how attached you can get to a specific mug it becomes part of your routine without even realising it.

That connection is probably why I’ve also fallen in love with pottery itself. I really enjoy making ceramics, especially cups, even though I’m still learning. The glazing process is my favourite part, the way colours shift, melt, and transform in the kiln is honestly kind of magical. You never know exactly how it will turn out, and that unpredictability is what makes each piece feel special.

This website is a collection of mugs that I’ve come across and really connected with. It’s a way of showcasing the ones I find interesting, beautiful, or inspiring, and sharing them with others. I want people to slow down and notice the details, the brush marks, the colour combinations, the forms and see how something as simple as a mug can actually be really expressive and full of character.
